The journey — click a screen

The boundary

Building What the proof of concept must do
  • Home: mic, live-caption listening, Done, processing, “Type instead.”
  • One input → many memories/reminders, with people and dates (recurrence labeled).
  • Review: edit, remove, explicit save. Nothing saved silently.
  • Home sections: Coming up · Recently remembered (hidden when empty).
  • Memories: browse, detail, edit, delete, Pending ⇄ Completed.
  • Ask Huf: voice or typed, grounded, status-aware, sources shown, honest when empty.
  • Persistence across refresh on one device. Both demo beats with real speech.
Deferred Implied, but not now
  • Search in Memories — Ask Huf already finds things.
  • Clarifying-question conversations — editing on Review covers mistakes.
  • Settings screen — only if a real need appears.
  • Notifications at due time — scope Later.
  • Persistent Ask Huf chat history — not needed to prove recall.
Non-goals Won’t do
  • Contacts · calendar sync · recurring notifications · wake word · accounts & sync.
  • Folders, tags, advanced filters.
  • Hardcoded demo responses — every answer comes from saved data.
Your decisions Rules for the whole build
  • Every visible button has a purpose and actually works. No settings icon.
  • No “which Sarah?” conversation — keep the person as spoken, never assume.
  • Reminders: Pending and Completed — “important for Huf’s accuracy.”
  • Accurate retrieval over a sophisticated chatbot.
  • Inter-style sans, flat surfaces, subtle radius; the mic is the signature.
Resolved Confirmed at review
  • Search stays deferred — Ask Huf is how you find things.
  • Ambiguous “Sarah”: kept as spoken, editable, never auto-matched.
